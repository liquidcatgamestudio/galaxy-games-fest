# **Space Hunters: Galaxy**

**Galaxy Hunters**
<br></br>
Space Hunters: Galaxy (Pre-Alpha)<br>
Space doesn't care how good you are. It just keeps challenging you.
<br></br>
This is a roguelite co-op survival game built in Unreal Engine where your job is simple and desperate: hold the line, and grow stronger faster than the dark can grow around you.
<br></br>
Every run is a gamble on your own escalation. Powerful skill upgrades lets you reshape your ship mid-fight. You can upgrade your fire power, increase your spaceship endurance, or just build up propulsors that can make you as fast as the light speed. Choose wisely in order to build a spaceship with a unique set of abilities and characteristics that make it powerful against the horde of the planet you are at.
<br></br>
Behind it, a two-person crew: Gabriel Borges on code, hunting the performance to keep hundreds of enemies alive and smooth; Felipe Squiers on 3D art, giving the fleet and the fury a look worth staring down.
<br></br>
Small team. Big swarms. One ship against the whole galaxy.
<br></br>
