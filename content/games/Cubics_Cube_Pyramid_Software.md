# **Cubics Cube**

**Pyramid Software**

Cubics Cube is a nostalgic blend of Bejeweled, Balatro, and the classic Rubik's Cube. Upgrade your cube as you progress through increasingly difficult rounds. Form patterns, complete faces, or solve the cube while experimenting with numerous cube-breaking modifiers and effects.
<br></br>
Cubics Cube also features an extensive virtual cubing mode, with various input methods and ways for you to interact with your cube. You can practice your cubing skills in zen mode, or fight for a spot on the leaderboards in our speed-solving mode!
<br></br>
