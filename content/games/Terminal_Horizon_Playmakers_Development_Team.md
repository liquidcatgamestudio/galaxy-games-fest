# **Terminal Horizon**

**Playmakers Development Team**

Terminal Horizon is a cyberpunk turn-based tactics roguelike. Lead a group of awakened simulants, ASIMs, in their escape from the dystopian megacity of New Damascus, and grant them their dying wish to see the stars for the first and last time. Fight against hostile simulants across three districts in turn-based grid combat, and combine abilities, passives, and over 100 unique items to create devastating, game-breaking builds.
