# **Multi-Merge Mayhem**

**PARTYBOMB STUDIO**
<br></br>
PARTY PLATFORMER CHAOS<br>
What do you get when you put a party platformer and a whole bunch of microgames in a blender? Multi-Merge Mayhem is a chaotic party game for up to 8 players where multiple microgames play at the same time. Run, jump, and punch your way through overlapping challenges as you fight to outscore your friends in pure, unpredictable mayhem!
<br></br>
MERGING MICROGAMES<br>
Multiple microgames can play at the same time! Dodge falling bricks, solve math equations, all while jumping over saw blades too?! With 20 different microgames and over 1140 unique microgame combinations, experience frantic, chaotic gameplay with heaps of replayability. No two matches will be the same!
<br></br>
CRAZY MODIFIERS<br>
Turn the screen upside-down, make everyone grow to a huge size, and even give everyone infinite jumps! Modifiers add an extra layer of chaos and unpredictability!
<br></br>
1-8 PLAYERS LOCAL/ONLINE<br>
Up to 8 player support for both local and online play! Play with your friends and family on the couch, or play together online through private lobbies or matchmaking. Also, just two buttons and a joystick to move, jump and attack! Accessible to players of all skill levels with simple controls!
<br></br>
