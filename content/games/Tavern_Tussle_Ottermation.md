# **Tavern Tussle**

**Ottermation**

After countless nights of the same old "put it on my tab!" at the tavern, you have racked up quite the debt. As tavern tradition dictates, those who are unable to pay their dues must pay through another form... ENTERTAINMENT! Participate in the wizard's games to entertain the fellow tavern goers against other broke adventurers, and hopefully you'll earn some coin along the way with your performance. If your endeavours prove successful, perhaps you'll win enough games to pay out of your debt... lest you pay through eternal servitude as a frog.
