# **Bio Rover**

**XZ**

Mars is dead, the colonies abandoned. You are a BioRover – a cybernetic Mars exploration rover with organic computing.
<br></br>
During your endless wander, you receive a signal from within the dunes, one which feels inexplicably familiar to you. You must find the source of this signal; it may be the last trace of humanity left on the planet.
<br></br>
About: BioRover is a narrative-driven sci-fi horror game inspired by Iron Lung where the player steps into the chassis of a sentient Mars rover. BioRover is set on a post-apocalyptic Mars where the once terraformed ecosystem has collapsed due to an endless sandstorm. The player must use their camera as their eyes to navigate the difficult Martian terrain and excavate various mysterious items to find what the source of a mysterious signal. Features approximately 10-15 minutes of gameplay.
<br></br>
Content Warning: low-fidelity images of animal remains, loneliness, death, general horror themes.
<br></br>
(The current version is an early prototype)
