# **Castillon**

**One up plus**

Castillon is a coop or single player RTS Tower defence hybrid set in a mystical land of magic. It is a short strategy game where you freely build and defend your castle with magic. Gather resources and explore during the day, then survive enemy attacks at night. Use four types of magic to create the best defence, all in about 45 minutes.

Build your castle by day and defend it at night!
