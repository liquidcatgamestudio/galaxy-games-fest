# **Nobody Hears**

**MuGui_Cinnamon**

It feels like just another ordinary day: the familiar room, the habitual conversations, and the gentle presence of your sister by your side.<br>
Yet you find yourself unable to move forward—the unspoken shadow never lifts from your supposedly peaceful life.<br>
If you pretend not to hear it, does it truly cease to exist?<br>
And yet, everything is happening right before your eyes, undeniably real.<br>
The words that should have been spoken… in the end, Nobody Hears.
<br></br>
“Nobody Hears” is a short multi-loop narrative game that blends exploration, puzzles, and environmental storytelling. Players will share a seemingly mundane daily life with their sister, uncovering subtle changes with each loop and slowly touching upon the truth hidden beneath routine.<br>
Within the repetition, you will experience emotions of struggle, suppression, and release. Breaking the cycle is not only a puzzle to solve but also a confrontation with—and understanding of—yourself.<br>
Through repeated exploration, you must notice and piece together the fine cracks in daily life, gradually assembling the deeper narrative beneath.<br>
As new paths are unlocked, the true thoughts and unspoken words of the sisters will rise to the surface.<br>
Will you accept the gentle illusion before you, or turn away after relentless struggle?<br>
In the end, can you pierce the fog of memory and face the unavoidable truth?<br>
The answer lies in every seemingly insignificant choice and action you take.
