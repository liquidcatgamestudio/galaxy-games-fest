# **Die Driver**

**Mt. Games**

Trees from the cursed 'Forest of the Dead' are rising, engulfing the world. It swallows and drowns anything that goes beneath its endless canopy. Like a sea of timber, it spreads with every life it claims. Necromancers, known as Die Casters, sail enchanted ships across its treetops before diving below to recover the spirits trapped within. But they'd best watch where they step... they wouldn't want it to wake up.
<br></br>
Die Diver is a solo-developed GameMaker Studio 2 project by Monty Coombs, begun in 2023, that explores the idea of dying, or 'die-ing', in roguelite games. Rather than traditional deckbuilding, Die Diver uses real-time dice-pool building, where players physically cast enchanted dice at their foes while pushing their luck to collect new dice before racing back to the safety of their ship. As their dice pool gradually grows, players specialise their collection with unique abilities and combinations, creating new strategies for every expedition beneath the canopy.
<br></br>
Inspired by games like The Binding of Isaac, Spelunky and Slay the Spire, Die Diver combines the tension of roguelites with the excitement of real-time and dungeon-crawling board games such as Escape: The Curse of the Temple and Nemesis, transforming tactile tabletop mechanics into a more accessible digital adventure.
<br></br>
Die Diver is made for players who love rolling a die, building a powerful dice pool, and, every now and then, dying in the pursuit of one more run.
