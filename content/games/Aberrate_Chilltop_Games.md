# **Aberrate**

**Chilltop Games**
<br></br>
Aberrate Inc. is a third-person 3D puzzle game where you guide N.T.R.N., a curious little robot, through tightly designed levels inside a secretive corporation.<br>
Winner of SXSW Sydney’s Best Student Game Award 2025, Aberrate Inc. focuses on deceptively simple logic puzzles that make you feel clever when they click.<br>
Help N.T.R.N. split and combine red and blue boxes to solve increasingly playful levels, while you delve deeper into the strange workings and mysterious history of Aberrate Inc.!
<br></br>
**DISCOVER, MANIPULATE, ABERRATE!**<br>
At the heart of Aberrate Inc. is your ability to SPLIT and COMBINE boxes.
In each level you’ll:<br>
ABERRATE to split boxes into distinct red and blue versions.<br>
Pick up, place, and FUSE your boxes in the right sequence to solve puzzle rooms.<br>
SWAP between box states freely, and from anywhere in the room.<br>
SOLVE puzzles using these mechanics to discover the world of Aberrate Inc!<br>
ABERRATE and FUSE always behave cleanly and predictably. However, as you progress, new discoveries (👀) may complicate how you reach a solution.
<br></br>
**KEY FEATURES**<br>
**Cozy, ‘thinky’ logic puzzles:** A tightly wound campaign of short, readable levels built around tactile, smart box interactions.<br>
**25+ handcrafted levels across 3 distinct chapters:** Guide N.T.R.N. through a structured campaign that steadily layers new twists on the core ABERRATE/FUSE mechanic. <br>
**SPLIT and COMBINE boxes:** Aberrate boxes into distinct red and blue versions, combine them, and learn how small changes in placement and ordering can reshape each solution. <br>
**Evolving complexity:** Discover additional aberration behaviours and quirks as you progress, adding satisfying depth to each puzzle. <br>
**Esoteric inhabitants:** Navigate levels as N.T.R.N. and uncover the backstory of Aberrate Inc. and its mysterious past.<br>
**Low-pressure play:** No timers, no cute robots in peril. Simply experiment freely as you chase that “oh, of course!” moment.
