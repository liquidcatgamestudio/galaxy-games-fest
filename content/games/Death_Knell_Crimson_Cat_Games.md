# **Death Knell**

**Crimson Cat Games**

Death Knell is a gothic, deckbuilding roguelike where your turn never ends. Each action brings the enemy's attack closer - but you're always in control. Conquer rival cults and monstrous horrors to ascend to the realm of godhood in a decaying, abandoned world.
<br></br>
**Story:** <br>
God has died -- leaving their Throne empty. Without them, the world is falling into ruin, with powerful entities forming cults in a bid to take the Throne. You are the Chosen of one such entity, and fated to leave this world behind as a pawn in the grand struggle.
<br></br>
**Current Features:**<br>
• **Roguelike Mode:** Encountered enemies and cards are randomized each time you play through the game. Currently contains 8 different normal fights, 2 bosses, and 20+ cards that you can upgrade.<br>
• **Variable turn system:** playing cards costs “time” instead of resources, and the actions of each enemy are tied to a timer. Turn order constantly changes and staggers as enemies take different actions, keeping you on your toes.<br>
• **Skills:** Your patron has granted you abilities that do not advance time when used, but require sacrificing cards from your hand. Find use for unwanted cards and pull off last-minute actions by using them instead.
