# **Cow Chess**

**Lablab**

Cow Chess has absolutely nothing to do with cows, or with chess.
<br></br>
A real-time artillery party game for up to 8 players. Think Super Smash Bros meets Worms, with a movement system all its own.
<br></br>
![](https://www.lablab.gg/cow_chess/gifs/Explosions%20-%204%20player.gif)
Stick to walls, swing from your tongue, and fire rockets to destroy the terrain under your opponents.
<br></br>
No timers, no health bars. Last cow standing wins.
<br></br>
Find out more at their Steam Page >> **[[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3444110/Cow_Chess/)]**.
<br></br>
Learn more about Cow Chess on their **[[website](https://www.lablab.gg/#cowchess)]** !
