# **CHOPSTICKS: Stick Figure Fighting**

**Linear Interpolation**

CHOPSTICKS: Stick Figure Fighting is a fighting game set across the globe. With just one attack button and a focus on parrying, strategy is key. Each map opens up unique gameplay opportunities with awesome map specific mechanics: from giant death lasers to sticks that can be used as swords!
<br></br>
In singleplayer, you'll fight through an epic campaign against enemies around the world in diverse locations (Including Sydney!), earning rewards along the way. You'll unlock new colours with unique personalities, and cool finishers to use against your friends!
<br></br>
CHOPSTICKS is designed to be easy for beginners to pick up, but features deep combat and counter play mechanics for players seeking a more in-depth experience.
<br></br>
CHOPSTICKS' animation was inspired by the golden age of internet stick figure fighting videos. All of our animations are hand drawn in silky smooth framerates for combat that feels like you're watching a classic stick figure animation. y loads of different game modes, maps and characters and ZOOOOM your way to the top!
