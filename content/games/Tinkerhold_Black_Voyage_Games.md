# **Tinkerhold**

**Black Voyage Games**

Your castle is a huge underground drill. You didn't mean to surface in insectoid territory. The insectoids don't care. Protect your castle, gather resources, upgrade your kit, and exterminate the insectoid Hivemind before the swarm overwhelms you.
<br></br>
Tinkerhold is a real-time top-down defense game. By day, roam the map, fight through wandering hostiles, raid enemy outposts for materials, and build up your fortress of towers around your Core. By night, insectoid hordes erupt from the ground and march on your walls, growing stronger every wave. Recruit followers, unlock new weapons and blueprints, and push deeper into hostile territory to find the enemy stronghold. Destroy it before the nights overwhelm you.
