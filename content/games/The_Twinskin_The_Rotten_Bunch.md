# **The Twinskin**

**The Rotten Bunch**

You're called to a small town to investigate a murder, but when everyone is accounted for and the body is charred beyond recognition you know it must be a case of the creature known as.. The Twinskin. Ask questions, cross reference evidence, and figure out which suspect isn't as they seem.
