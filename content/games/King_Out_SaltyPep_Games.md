# **KING OUT**

**SaltyPep Games**
<br></br>
KING OUT is a 1v1 online multiplayer Chess-Boxing game where players muster the strategy within chaos. Draft your pieces, navigate your moves in chess, then nominate your piece to become a fighter, and box on the board against your opponent. Capture or K.O. your opponent’s last King to win!
