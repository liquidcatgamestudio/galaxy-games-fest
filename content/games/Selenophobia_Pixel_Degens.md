# **Selenophobia**

**Pixel Degen**

Selenophobia (/səˌlinəˈfoʊbiə/) - A fear of the moon.

A puzzle platformer where moonlight is both your guide and your greatest threat. Each level is played twice, the same moonlit world feels entirely different depending on who you are.

As White, the light is your ally, you control the environment. As Black, stepping into the moonlight means instant death, forcing you to navigate the shadows, carefully timing your movements, and use the environment to block the beam that hunts you.

Solve each level twice, see it from both sides, and discover that the solution for one may be the undoing of the other.
