# **Angela Sae-Lam (She/They)**

Angela is a 3D artist and trainer with a diverse background centered around animation, education and community-building. 

They are currently a 3D Props Modeller in children's TV and University Lecturer in 3D modelling and Digital Sculpting. On the side, they volunteer for SIGGRAPH as Communications Officer, indie games as an Asset Artist and animated shorts as Asset Lead/ Supervisor to contribute to community-centric initiatives connecting people and learning opportunities. 

They seek to incubate and explore new and unconventional opportunities for collaboration between creatives, tech and education as a means to extend the lifespan and reach of transferable skills rather than job titles.
