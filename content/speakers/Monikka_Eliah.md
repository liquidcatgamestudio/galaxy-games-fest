# **Monikka Eliah (She/Her)**

Monikka Eliah is an Assyrian writer, visual artist. She has worked in prose, theatre and screen. Her work has been
published in online literary journals and several anthologies. In 2026 she participated in Beyond the Script, a program that supported artists from Western Sydney develop their narrative game design skills.
