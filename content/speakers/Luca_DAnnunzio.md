# **Luca D'Annunzio (He/him)**

Luca is an indie game developer currently working on a small pixel art platformer, and a co-op action game. He has experience as an animator and designer on Insignia, an upcoming title releasing on Steam. Coming into game development with a background in graphic design, animation, and mostly self taught programming, Luca is an active member of the NSW game dev community and regularly attends local meetups.
