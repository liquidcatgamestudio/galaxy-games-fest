# **Dr. Jaime Garcia (He/Him)**

**Dr. Jaime Garcia** is an Associate Professor with research interests in serious games, gamification, and digital health. His work explores how game-based technologies can support health, education, and wellbeing. He teaches in game development programs and collaborates with researchers and industry partners on projects that apply games to real-world challenges.
