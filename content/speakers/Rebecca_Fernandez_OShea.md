# **Rebecca Fernandez O'Shea (She/Her)**

Rebecca Fernandez O'Shea is a senior graphics programmer at Nixxes Software, part of PlayStation Studios. Rebecca's career in the games industry spans almost 15 years and 3 different countries She has volunteered and worked as an event co-ordinator, teacher, indie developer and now in AAA.
