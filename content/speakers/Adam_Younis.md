# **Adam Younis (He/him)**

Adam Younis is a game developer based in Sydney, best known for creating educational content on game production, art, and design. His Twitch and YouTube channels feature tutorials, devlogs, and tips on game development, with a focus on pixel art and 2D games.

Adam’s work centres around his project Insignia, an action-adventure title, which he began as a solo developer. The project, planned for release on PC and consoles, has received grant funding from Screen Australia and Screen NSW. Adam continues to live stream the game’s development, currently directing a team of 6 through production.
